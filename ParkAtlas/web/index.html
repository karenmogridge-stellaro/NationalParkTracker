Celebrate every visit

Each new park earns you a little something — Trailhead, Day Hiker, Ranger, Pathfinder and beyond. First-time parks get confetti.

Know before you go

Curated trails with distances, camping and fee details, directions, and NPS links. Tap a trail to log it. Only remember the year? Log just that.

Parks near you

Open the app and see national and state parks within reach that you haven't explored yet. One tap adds them to your wishlist for the next free weekend.

Go together

Follow friends, send a High-Five when they log a park, and share your own park cards to Stories. Link Strava and hikes log themselves.

Why I built this

Hi, I'm Ren.

I made ParkAtlas for myself first — a simple way to keep track of the parks I've been to, and a nudge to get outside more often than I otherwise would.

After losing my husband of twenty years to cancer, I stopped believing that what matters most is time behind a desk. What matters is the trail, the people you walk it with, and actually remembering the days you spent out there.

So that's what this app is for. Not the number — the getting out. I want to surround myself with people who feel the same way, and I'd love for you to be one of them.

Hope to see you on the trails. — Ren
