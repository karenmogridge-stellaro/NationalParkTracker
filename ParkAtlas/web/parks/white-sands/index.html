White Sands

Glistening white gypsum dunes stretch across the Tularosa Basin in a surreal landscape unlike anywhere on Earth.

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – March. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Las Cruces, NM. Park center: 32.780°, -106.170°.
