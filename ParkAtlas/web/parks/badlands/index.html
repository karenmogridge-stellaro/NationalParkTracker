Best time to visit

Peak season: June – August. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Rapid City, SD. Park center: 43.850°, -102.340°.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Badlands National Park?

Entry is $30 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Badlands?

Peak season is June – August. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Badlands National Park?

Yes — about 198 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Badlands National Park?

Rapid City, SD is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Badlands?

Popular trails include Notch Trail (1.5 mi), Door Trail (0.8 mi), Window Trail (0.3 mi), Saddle Pass Trail (0.3 mi). The park has roughly 11 named trails.
