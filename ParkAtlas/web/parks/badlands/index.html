Badlands

Dramatic layered rock formations and fossil beds across the South Dakota prairie.

Camping

Approximately 198 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – August).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – August. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Rapid City, SD. Park center: 43.850°, -102.340°.
