Indiana Dunes

Sand dunes, beaches, bogs, and prairies along southern Lake Michigan, just one hour from Chicago.

🥾 50+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 83 campsites ☀️ May – September 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Indiana Dunes has roughly 50 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 83 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – September).

Find places to stay near Indiana Dunes →

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Chicago, IL. Park center: 41.650°, -87.050°.
