Haleakalā National Park

A massive dormant shield volcano on Maui, with a 7-mile wide summit crater and the Road to Hana.

Peak season: Year-round. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Haleakalā National Park?

Entry is $30 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Haleakalā?

Peak season is Year-round. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Haleakalā National Park?

Yes — about 105 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Haleakalā National Park?

Kahului, HI is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Haleakalā?

Popular trails include Sliding Sands Trail (11.0 mi), Halemaumau Trail (2.0 mi), Crater Trail (2.2 mi), Hosmer Grove Trail (0.5 mi). The park has roughly 30 named trails.
