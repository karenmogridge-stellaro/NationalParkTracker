Guadalupe Mountains

Remote West Texas mountains rising from desert, home to Guadalupe Peak — Texas's highest summit.

🥾 80+ trails 🎟️ $25 / vehicle ⛺ 40 campsites ☀️ March – May, Sept – Nov 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 40 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (March – May, Sept – Nov).

Best time to visit

Peak season: March – May, Sept – Nov. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: El Paso, TX. Park center: 31.920°, -104.870°.
