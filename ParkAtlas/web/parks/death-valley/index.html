Death Valley

America's largest national park outside Alaska — the hottest, driest place on the continent.

🥾 785+ trails 🎟️ $30 / vehicle ⛺ 1000 campsites ☀️ October – April 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 1000 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (October – April).

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Las Vegas, NV. Park center: 36.240°, -116.820°.
