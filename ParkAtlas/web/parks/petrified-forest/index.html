Petrified Forest

Colorful badlands and the world's largest collection of Late Triassic fossils and petrified wood.

🥾 18+ trails 🎟️ $25 / vehicle ⛺ No campgrounds ☀️ April – October 📅 Open year-round

Camping

There are no NPS campgrounds inside the park. Lodging and private campgrounds are typically available nearby.

Best time to visit

Peak season: April – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Gallup, NM. Park center: 34.980°, -109.780°.
