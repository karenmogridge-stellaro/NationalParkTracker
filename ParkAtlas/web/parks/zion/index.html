Zion

Towering red canyon walls and the famous Narrows crack through the high plateaus of southwest Utah.

🥾 90+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 280 campsites ☀️ March – May, Sept – Nov 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 280 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (March – May, Sept – Nov).

Best time to visit

Peak season: March – May, Sept – Nov. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Las Vegas, NV. Park center: 37.300°, -113.000°.
