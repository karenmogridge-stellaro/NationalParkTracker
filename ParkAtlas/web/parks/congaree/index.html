South Carolina · National Park

Congaree

Old-growth bottomland hardwood forest and the largest intact tract of southern floodplain forest.

🥾 25+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ No campgrounds ☀️ October – April 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Congaree has roughly 25 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

There are no NPS campgrounds inside the park. Lodging and private campgrounds are typically available nearby.

Find places to stay near Congaree →

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Columbia, SC. Park center: 33.790°, -80.780°.
