Mesa Verde

Over 5,000 ancestral Puebloan archaeological sites, including the iconic Cliff Palace.

🥾 80+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 267 campsites ☀️ May – September 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 267 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Durango, CO. Park center: 37.180°, -108.490°.
