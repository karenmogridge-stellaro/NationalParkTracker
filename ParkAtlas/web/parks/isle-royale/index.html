Michigan · National Park

Isle Royale

Remote backcountry island in Lake Superior accessible only by ferry or floatplane — wolves and moose inhabit its wilderness.

🥾 165+ trails 🎟️ $7 / person / day ⛺ 246 campsites ☀️ June – August 📅 Seasonal access

Trails

Isle Royale has roughly 165 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 246 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – August).

Find places to stay near Isle Royale →

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – August. Access is seasonal — confirm opening and closing dates on NPS.gov before you go.

Getting there

Nearest city: Houghton, MI. Park center: 48.100°, -88.550°.
