Gateway Arch National Park

About Gateway Arch

Gateway Arch National Park commemorates President Jefferson’s vision of a continental nation, the individuals and cultural groups who helped shape its history, St. Louis’ role in westward expansion, and Dred and Harriet Scott, who sued for their freedom in the Old Courthouse.

Things to do: Astronomy · Stargazing · Biking · Road Biking · Food · Dining · Picnicking · Guided Tours · Boat Tour · Junior Ranger Program · Park Film · Museum Exhibits · Shopping · Bookstore and Park Store

Best time to visit

Peak season: Year-round. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.
