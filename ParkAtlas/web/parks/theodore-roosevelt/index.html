Theodore Roosevelt

Colorful badlands where Theodore Roosevelt came to grieve — and found his love of conservation.

Camping

Approximately 146 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Bismarck, ND. Park center: 46.980°, -103.450°.
