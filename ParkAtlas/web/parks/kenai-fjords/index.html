Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Kenai Fjords National Park?

Entry is Free. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Kenai Fjords?

Peak season is May – September. Access is seasonal; check NPS.gov for opening dates.

Can you camp in Kenai Fjords National Park?

Yes — about 12 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Kenai Fjords National Park?

Seward, AK is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Kenai Fjords?

Popular trails include Exit Glacier Trail (3.5 mi), Harding Icefield Trail (8.2 mi), Coastal Walk (2.0 mi). The park has roughly 12 named trails.
