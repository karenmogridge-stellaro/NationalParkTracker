Virgin Islands National Park

Pristine beaches, coral reefs, and tropical forest on St. John in the US Virgin Islands.

🥾 20+ trails 🎟️ $8 / person ⛺ 44 campsites ☀️ December – April 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Virgin Islands has roughly 20 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 44 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (December – April).

Find places to stay near Virgin Islands →

Best time to visit

Peak season: December – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Charlotte Amalie, VI. Park center: 18.330°, -64.730°.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Virgin Islands National Park?

Entry is $8 / person. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Virgin Islands?

Peak season is December – April. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Virgin Islands National Park?

Yes — about 44 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Virgin Islands National Park?

Charlotte Amalie, VI is the nearest major city.
