Rocky Mountain

Trail Ridge Road crosses the Continental Divide through high tundra and 114 named peaks over 10,000 feet.

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Denver, CO. Park center: 40.400°, -105.580°.
