Bryce Canyon

Crimson hoodoos and amphitheaters of natural rock spires at high elevation in southern Utah.

🥾 15+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 218 campsites ☀️ May – September 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 218 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: St. George, UT. Park center: 37.570°, -112.180°.
