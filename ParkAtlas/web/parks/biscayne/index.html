Biscayne

Mostly underwater — turquoise bays, mangrove forests, and living coral reefs near Miami.

🥾 2+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 64 campsites ☀️ December – April 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Biscayne has roughly 2 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 64 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (December – April).

Find places to stay near Biscayne →

Best time to visit

Peak season: December – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Miami, FL. Park center: 25.650°, -80.080°.
