West Virginia · National Park

New River Gorge

Stunning gorge of the New River, one of the oldest rivers in the world, with world-class whitewater.

🥾 112+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 100 campsites ☀️ April – October 📅 Open year-round

Trails

New River Gorge has roughly 112 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 100 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (April – October).

Find places to stay near New River Gorge →

Best time to visit

Peak season: April – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Charleston, WV. Park center: 37.870°, -81.080°.
