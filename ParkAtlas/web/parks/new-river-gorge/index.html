New River Gorge National Park

Stunning gorge of the New River, one of the oldest rivers in the world, with world-class whitewater.

🥾 112+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 100 campsites ☀️ April – October 📅 Open year-round

Trails

New River Gorge has roughly 112 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 100 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (April – October).

Find places to stay near New River Gorge →

Best time to visit

Peak season: April – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Charleston, WV. Park center: 37.870°, -81.080°.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit New River Gorge National Park?

Entry is Free. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit New River Gorge?

Peak season is April – October. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in New River Gorge National Park?

Yes — about 100 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to New River Gorge National Park?

Charleston, WV is the nearest major city.
