Arkansas · National Park

Hot Springs

Historic bathhouses line Bathhouse Row above 47 naturally hot thermal springs in the Ouachita Mountains.

🥾 26+ trails 🎟️ $20 / vehicle ⛺ 26 campsites ☀️ April – October 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Hot Springs has roughly 26 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 26 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (April – October).

Find places to stay near Hot Springs →

Best time to visit

Peak season: April – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Hot Springs, AR. Park center: 34.510°, -93.050°.
