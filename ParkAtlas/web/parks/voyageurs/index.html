Voyageurs

A wild waterway — four large interconnected lakes on the Canadian border accessible only by boat.

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – August. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: International Falls, MN. Park center: 48.480°, -92.840°.
