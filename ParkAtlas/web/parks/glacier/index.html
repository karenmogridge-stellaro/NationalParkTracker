Camping

Approximately 1000 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (July – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: July – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Kalispell, MT. Park center: 48.600°, -113.800°.
