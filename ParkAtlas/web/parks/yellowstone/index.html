Yellowstone

The world's first national park — supervolcano, geysers, hot springs, and America's largest bison herd.

Camping

Approximately 2150 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Jackson, WY. Park center: 44.420°, -110.590°.
