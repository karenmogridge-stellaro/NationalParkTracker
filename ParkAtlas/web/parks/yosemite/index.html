Yosemite

Glacier-carved valley of waterfalls, granite cliffs, and giant sequoias — one of America's most iconic landscapes.

Camping

Approximately 1400 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Merced, CA. Park center: 37.870°, -119.550°.
