Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Yosemite National Park?

Entry is $35 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Yosemite?

Peak season is May – September. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Yosemite National Park?

Yes — about 1400 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Yosemite National Park?

Merced, CA is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Yosemite?

Popular trails include Mist Trail to Vernal Fall (5.5 mi), Half Dome Trail (17.0 mi), Valley Floor Loop Trail (7.2 mi), Mirror Lake Loop (5.0 mi). The park has roughly 800 named trails.
