Everglades

A slow-moving river of grass — the largest subtropical wilderness in the US.

🥾 15+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 234 campsites ☀️ December – April 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 234 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (December – April).

Best time to visit

Peak season: December – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Miami, FL. Park center: 25.390°, -80.930°.
