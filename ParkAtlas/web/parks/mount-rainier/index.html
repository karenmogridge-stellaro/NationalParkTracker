Mount Rainier

A glaciated stratovolcano surrounded by subalpine meadows, old-growth forests, and the iconic Wonderland Trail.

Camping

Approximately 534 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (July – August).

Best time to visit

Peak season: July – August. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Seattle, WA. Park center: 46.850°, -121.750°.
