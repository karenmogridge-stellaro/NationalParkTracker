Big Bend

Remote Chihuahuan Desert wilderness along the Rio Grande, with canyons and hot springs.

🥾 150+ trails 🎟️ $30 / vehicle ⛺ 150 campsites ☀️ October – April 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 150 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (October – April).

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: El Paso, TX. Park center: 29.250°, -103.250°.
