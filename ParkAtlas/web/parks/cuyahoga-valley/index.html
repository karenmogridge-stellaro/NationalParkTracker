Cuyahoga Valley

Scenic river valley, waterfalls, and historic villages between Cleveland and Akron.

Camping

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Cleveland, OH. Park center: 41.240°, -81.550°.
