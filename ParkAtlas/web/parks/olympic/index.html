Three distinct ecosystems — glacier-capped peaks, temperate rainforest, and 73 miles of wild coastline.

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Nearest city: Seattle, WA. Park center: 47.970°, -123.500°.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Olympic National Park?

Entry is $30 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Olympic?

Peak season is June – September. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Olympic National Park?

Yes — about 900 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Olympic National Park?

Seattle, WA is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Olympic?

Popular trails include Hurricane Ridge Trail (3.0 mi), Hoh Rain Forest Loop (0.8 mi), Sol Duc Falls Trail (1.6 mi), Quinault Rain Forest Loop (0.4 mi). The park has roughly 611 named trails.
