Approximately 900 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Seattle, WA. Park center: 47.970°, -123.500°.
