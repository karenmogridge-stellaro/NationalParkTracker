Capitol Reef

Massive white domes of Navajo Sandstone fold and twist above colorful cliffs in south-central Utah.

🥾 14+ trails 🎟️ $20 / vehicle ⛺ 71 campsites ☀️ March – May, Sept – Oct 📅 Open year-round

Best time to visit

Peak season: March – May, Sept – Oct. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Torrey, UT. Park center: 38.090°, -111.150°.
