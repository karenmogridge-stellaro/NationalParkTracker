Grand Canyon

One of the great wonders of the world — 277 miles of canyon and a mile deep, carved by the Colorado River.

🥾 800+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 700 campsites ☀️ March – May, Sept – Nov 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 700 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (March – May, Sept – Nov).

Best time to visit

Peak season: March – May, Sept – Nov. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Flagstaff, AZ. Park center: 36.060°, -112.140°.
