Acadia National Park

Granite peaks, rocky coastline, and quiet forests on Mount Desert Island. Home to the iconic carriage roads and Cadillac Mountain.

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Bangor, ME. Park center: 44.350°, -68.210°.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Acadia National Park?

Entry is $35 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Acadia?

Peak season is June – September. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Acadia National Park?

Yes — about 530 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Acadia National Park?

Bangor, ME is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Acadia?

Popular trails include Cadillac Mountain South Ridge Trail (7.4 mi), Ocean Path (4.2 mi), Precipice Trail (3.3 mi), Jordan Pond Path (3.3 mi). The park has roughly 45 named trails.
