Crater Lake National Park

The deepest lake in the US, formed in the caldera of an ancient volcano, known for its vivid blue water.

Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Crater Lake National Park?

Entry is $30 / vehicle. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Crater Lake?

Peak season is July – September. The park is open year-round, with some roads and facilities on reduced winter schedules.

Can you camp in Crater Lake National Park?

Yes — about 377 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Crater Lake National Park?

Medford, OR is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Crater Lake?

Popular trails include Rim Drive (33.0 mi), Cleetwood Cove Trail (2.2 mi), Watchman Peak Trail (1.5 mi), Garfield Peak Trail (3.4 mi). The park has roughly 18 named trails.
