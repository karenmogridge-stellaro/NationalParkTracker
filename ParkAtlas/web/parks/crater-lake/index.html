Crater Lake

The deepest lake in the US, formed in the caldera of an ancient volcano, known for its vivid blue water.

Best time to visit

Peak season: July – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Medford, OR. Park center: 42.940°, -122.100°.
