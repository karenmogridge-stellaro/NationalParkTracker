Canyonlands

Vast network of canyons, mesas, and buttes carved by the Colorado and Green rivers.

🥾 20+ trails 🎟️ $35 / vehicle ⛺ 30 campsites ☀️ March – May, Sept – Nov 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 30 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (March – May, Sept – Nov).

Best time to visit

Peak season: March – May, Sept – Nov. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Moab, UT. Park center: 38.200°, -109.930°.
