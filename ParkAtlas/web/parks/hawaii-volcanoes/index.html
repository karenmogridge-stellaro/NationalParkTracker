Hawaiʻi Volcanoes

One of the world's most active volcanoes — lava tubes, craters, and ongoing eruptions on the Big Island.

Camping

Approximately 168 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (Year-round).

Best time to visit

Peak season: Year-round. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Hilo, HI. Park center: 19.380°, -155.200°.
