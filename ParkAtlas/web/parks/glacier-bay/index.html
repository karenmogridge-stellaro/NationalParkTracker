Glacier Bay

A living laboratory of glacier retreat — tidewater glaciers calve into the bay daily.

🥾 5+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ No campgrounds ☀️ June – August 📅 Seasonal access

Trails

Glacier Bay has roughly 5 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

There are no NPS campgrounds inside the park. Lodging and private campgrounds are typically available nearby.

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – August. Access is seasonal — confirm opening and closing dates on NPS.gov before you go.

Getting there

Nearest city: Juneau, AK. Park center: 58.660°, -136.000°.
