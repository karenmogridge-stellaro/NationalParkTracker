Nevada · National Park

Great Basin

One of the least-visited parks — caves, bristlecone pines, and one of Nevada's highest peaks.

🥾 58+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 170 campsites ☀️ June – October 📅 Open year-round

Trails

Great Basin has roughly 58 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 170 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – October).

Find places to stay near Great Basin →

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Ely, NV. Park center: 38.980°, -114.300°.
