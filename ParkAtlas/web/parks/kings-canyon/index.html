Kings Canyon

Deep granite canyons, giant sequoias, and hundreds of miles of wilderness trails in the Sierra Nevada.

Camping

Approximately 400 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – October).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Fresno, CA. Park center: 36.880°, -118.550°.
