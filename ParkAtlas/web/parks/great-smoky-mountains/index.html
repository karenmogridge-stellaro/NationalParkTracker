Great Smoky Mountains

America's most visited park — ancient mountains blanketed in old-growth forest and wildflowers.

Camping

Approximately 931 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – October).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Knoxville, TN. Park center: 35.610°, -83.530°.
