North Cascades

Some of North America's most rugged alpine terrain — over 300 glaciers and 400 miles of trails.

🥾 400+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 400 campsites ☀️ July – September 📅 Open year-round

Trails

North Cascades has roughly 400 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 400 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (July – September).

Find places to stay near North Cascades →

Best time to visit

Peak season: July – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Bellingham, WA. Park center: 48.700°, -121.200°.
