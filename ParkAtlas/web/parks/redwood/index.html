Redwood

The world's tallest trees — coast redwoods soaring over 350 feet — in ancient forests along the Northern California coast.

🥾 200+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 600 campsites ☀️ May – September 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 600 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – September. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Eureka, CA. Park center: 41.300°, -124.000°.
