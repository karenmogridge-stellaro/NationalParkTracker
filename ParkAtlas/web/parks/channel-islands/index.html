Channel Islands

Five rugged islands off the coast of Southern California, home to unique wildlife and kelp forests.

🥾 35+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 60 campsites ☀️ June – October 📅 Open year-round

Camping

Approximately 60 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (June – October).

Best time to visit

Peak season: June – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Ventura, CA. Park center: 34.010°, -119.420°.
