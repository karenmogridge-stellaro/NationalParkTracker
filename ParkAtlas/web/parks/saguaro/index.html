Saguaro

Giant saguaro cacti — tall as houses — stand across two park units flanking the city of Tucson.

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – April. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Tucson, AZ. Park center: 32.250°, -110.500°.
