Where the Mojave and Colorado deserts meet — iconic twisted Joshua Trees amid boulder piles and starlit skies.

Camping

Approximately 500 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (October – May).

Best time to visit

Peak season: October – May. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Palm Springs, CA. Park center: 33.880°, -115.900°.
