Katmai

Famous for brown bears fishing for salmon at Brooks Falls — a vast volcanic landscape in Alaska.

🥾 9+ trails 🎟️ Free ⛺ 60 campsites ☀️ July – September 📅 Seasonal access

Trails

Katmai has roughly 9 named trails. Log the one you hiked in the app — or just the park if you're not sure.

Camping

Approximately 60 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (July – September).

Best time to visit

Peak season: July – September. Access is seasonal — confirm opening and closing dates on NPS.gov before you go.

Getting there

Nearest city: King Salmon, AK. Park center: 58.500°, -154.970°.
