Shenandoah

Skyline Drive winds 105 miles along the Blue Ridge crest, with waterfalls, black bears, and fall foliage.

Camping

Approximately 567 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reservations are strongly recommended in peak season (May – October).

Best time to visit

Peak season: May – October. The park is open year-round, though some roads and facilities close or run reduced hours in winter.

Getting there

Nearest city: Washington DC. Park center: 38.490°, -78.350°.
