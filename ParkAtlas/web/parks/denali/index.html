Common questions

How much does it cost to visit Denali National Park?

Entry is $15 / person. An America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance fees at every national park.

When is the best time to visit Denali?

Peak season is June – August. Access is seasonal; check NPS.gov for opening dates.

Can you camp in Denali National Park?

Yes — about 330 campsites across the park's campgrounds. Reserve ahead in peak season.

What is the closest city to Denali National Park?

Fairbanks, AK is the nearest major city.

What are the best hikes in Denali?

Popular trails include Mount Healy Overlook Trail (4.7 mi), Triple Lakes Trail (8.9 mi), Horseshoe Lake Trail (3.0 mi), Rock Creek Trail (2.0 mi). The park has roughly 39 named trails.
