The Field Journal Note

Spanning nearly a million acres, Olympic National Park protects a vast wilderness, thousands of years of human history, and several distinct ecosystems, including glacier-capped mountains, old-growth temperate rainforests, and over 70 miles of wild coastline. This particular route takes you through the heart of the Hoh Rainforest before ascending toward the alpine meadows.
