Grand Teton National Park — #10 of the 63 for me. July 2025.

Breathtaking, and in a few spots slightly terrifying. In a good way.

🐻 A mama bear and her twin cubs on the trail ahead of us. We did not ask for a family photo. We quietly gave them the trail, the hill, and anything else they wanted.
🦌 “Hollywood,” the local celebrity elk, posing like he knows exactly how good he looks.
🏔 Paintbrush Divide: ~20 miles, ~4,500 ft of climbing, one very long and very perfect day.
🧊 The ice shelf just below the divide that we scooted across — poles planted, not looking down. Slide 6. Yes, that’s the trail.
⛺ Camped at Signal Mountain on Jackson Lake. Mount Moran out the front door every morning. Can’t recommend it enough.

Next summer I’m hoping to do this whole trip again in a sweet camper van from @geotrekvans. Manifesting.
