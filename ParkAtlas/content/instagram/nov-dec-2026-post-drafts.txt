It’s been snowing in Utah’s mountains, and there’s more on the way! Good reminder of my winter park rule: pick the backup plan before I leave home.

I check the park’s official alerts and road conditions, look up sunset and set a turnaround time, and make sure the trailhead or visitor center I’m counting on is actually open.

Then I pick a shorter backup hike. I’d rather make that call at home than from a parking lot with one bar of service.
